Where to buy books in Portuguese

Adapted from the New College Oxford Portuguese reading list.

In Portugal and Brazil

The best place to buy books in Portuguese — new or secondhand — is in Portugal or Brazil. In Portugal the main chains are FNAC and Bertrand (the oldest surviving bookshop in the world). Both countries have a wide network of secondhand shops: sebos in Brazil, alfarrabistas in Portugal. In Brazil, Livraria Travessa is a reliable independent chain. Bertrand and FNAC both offer worldwide online ordering.

In the UK

Grant & Cutler at Foyles (113–119 Charing Cross Road, London WC2H 0EB) is the main specialist for books in European languages, including Portuguese. They also offer a mail order service. Alternatively, The Portuguese Bookshop — part of the European Bookshop at 123 Gloucester Road, London SW7 4TE — sells Portuguese-language books and courses online and in store.

Elsewhere in Europe

Librairie Portugaise et Brésilienne in Paris (rue Tournefort, 5th arrondissement) is one of the only specialist Lusophone bookshops in Europe — the source for this catalogue — and sells online. A Livraria in Berlin (Torstraße 159) stocks books from Portugal and Brazil and offers a mail order service.

Online and secondhand

AbeBooks and Estante Virtual (Brazil's main secondhand marketplace) are good for out-of-print or cheaper editions. Amazon occasionally lists books in Portuguese but stock is inconsistent — worth checking, but don't pay a premium if other options exist.
