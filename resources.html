Resources

Reading lists, library guides, and learner resources for Lusophone literature.

A working list of external resources — academic reading lists, library guides, course syllabuses, and learner communities — for anyone trying to get further into Lusophone literature. Additions welcome via Suggest a book.

Category Resource What it covers Best for
Academic reading list Oxford (Wadham): Portuguese Reading List Curated literature list spanning Portuguese, Brazilian, and Lusophone texts, with translations where available. Learners who want a serious, canon-aware path into Lusophone literature.
Library guide Oxford Bodleian: Portuguese and Brazilian Language and Literature Broad guide to books and online resources for Portuguese, Brazil, and the Lusophone world. Finding structured background material and further reading.
Library guide Oxford Bodleian: Books Books-focused companion page for Portuguese and Brazilian literature resources. Browsing book-oriented academic resources.
Research guide UW–Madison: Lusophone Literature Resources Organised research guide covering Brazil, Portugal, Lusophone Africa, and East Timor — books, journals, and audiovisual resources. Building a wider reading and research pathway.
Course reading list New College Oxford: Preliminary Examination in Portuguese Course list for "An Introduction to Twentieth Century Lusophone Literature." PDF from 2017 — most recent publicly available version. Seeing what an academic programme treats as foundational.
Course syllabus HUJI: Introduction to Portuguese Literature⚠ Direct syllabus URL not confirmed — try the Rothberg course catalogue. Syllabus-style list for introductory Portuguese literature study at Hebrew University of Jerusalem. Comparing an international university canon.
Course reading list BYU: Luso-Brazilian Literatures MA reading list University reading list for Luso-Brazilian literature study (2022–23). Getting a broader Lusophone survey.
Learner community Practice Portuguese forum: Reading resources thread Community-curated thread of reading recommendations and book suggestions for learners at various levels. Finding approachable recommendations from other learners.
Learner blog LusoBritish: Best books and audiobooks for learning Portuguese Curated learner-oriented reviews of books and audiobooks for Portuguese study. Informal but accessible suggestions for self-study.
